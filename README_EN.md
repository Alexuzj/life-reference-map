# CANZHAO 参照

**When you feel lost, do not rush to find an answer. Find a few lives that give you something real to compare against.**

[中文](README.md) · [Copy the English prompt](prompt-en.md) · [复制中文 Prompt](prompt-cn.md)

## What is CANZHAO?

I have always been interested in many things: culture, art, food, wine, cities, and aesthetics.

But when I started thinking seriously about my career and life, having many interests became confusing. Could these interests belong together? Had anyone else turned a similarly mixed set of interests into a clear and meaningful path?

I realized that I did not need more generic advice. I needed people I could learn from.

CANZHAO is a prompt you can use with ChatGPT, Claude, DeepSeek, or another AI. It asks a few questions, builds a provisional picture of how you may want to work and live, and suggests three to five real people worth exploring.

They are not perfect models or definitive answers. They are starting points: people whose choices, mistakes, and paths may help you see possibilities that were previously invisible.

## Who is it for?

- People considering a career change without knowing what comes next
- People with many interests who do not fit neatly into one job title
- People who feel anxious about the future and lack relevant examples nearby
- People who want to explore their direction without taking another personality test

## How to use it

1. Open and copy the [English prompt](prompt-en.md).
2. Paste it into the AI you normally use.
3. Answer with real experiences, not only your ideal self.
4. Keep the two or three people you genuinely want to study further.

The process takes about **15–20 minutes**.

## Before you begin

CANZHAO cannot choose your life for you or guarantee the “right” role models. It offers a set of hypotheses worth testing.

The useful part is not the final list. It is noticing why certain lives attract you—and what that reveals about the life you may want to build.

AI can also make mistakes. The prompt asks it to label unverified information, but you should still check biographical details, books, interviews, and links. Do not treat an AI response as a fact by itself.

## Why the name CANZHAO?

“参照” means a point of reference.

The people you find are not idols or templates. You do not need to copy their lives. Their paths simply help you see what might be possible.
